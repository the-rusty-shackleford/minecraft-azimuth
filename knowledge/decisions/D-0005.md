# D-0005 — Players are heads at any distance, fading as the old bar did

Accepted 2026-09-24, after 1.0.1 went live. Rusty, in play: "The bar should still show players
at any distance, and fade in as you get closer like the old bar (villages and other points of
interest should not follow this, they should stay the same)."

D-0003 had drawn a player past the fade's floor (125 blocks) as a small outlined dot at the
floor's strength. In play that reads as the player being gone: a 5 px dot at 40 % over the dark
bar is almost nothing, where the Locator Bar kept the head at 40 % and let it brighten as you
closed in. The rule is the old one again: every player in the dimension is their framed head at
any distance, drawn by the fade curve of D-0001 (full to 50 blocks, easing to the floor at 125,
the floor from there), the server's `players.fade_*` still travelling with the payload. The far
dot and `Fade.far` are gone; `bar.heads = false` still draws plain dots for everyone.

Places are untouched: within `locations.range`, fading in over the last `locations.fade`
blocks, gone beyond. A village 300 blocks away is not on the bar; a player 300 blocks away is.

Amends D-0003. Verified by the booth: the Rover, 200 blocks off, is a head at the floor's
strength, measured against the computed 40 % over the bar's body, beside the near Surveyor at
near-full.
