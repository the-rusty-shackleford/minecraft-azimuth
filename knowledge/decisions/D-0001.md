# D-0001 — Our own bar, cloning what players know and nothing they don't

Accepted 2026-09-24.

Rusty first asked for atlas icons (villages, points of interest) on the Locator Bar the pack
ships (FuzjaJadrowa's 1.2.1, GPL), fading in within about 256 blocks. Reading that mod settled
it: no API, every marker class private, waypoints drawn as text symbols in tinted boxes with no
fade, and its bar overlapping the boss bars Chunky's pregeneration shows. Rusty: "I think it
might be easier for us to create our own version of the locator bar (call it Azimuth) that
clones the same functionality for players and adds an integration with the atlas."

**What is cloned**, so the bar players see does not change under them: a bar across the top of
the screen; the other players in the viewer's dimension as their heads, nearest first, at most
16, never anyone crouching, in spectator, invisible or wearing a pumpkin or a skull; heads fading
from full at 50 blocks to 40 % at 125 and no further (the pack ran the "no distance limit"
build, so nobody ever vanished); north, east, south and west marked; the viewer's coordinates
written under the bar; lodestone compasses in the inventory and, with a recovery compass, the
last death pointed at. Rusty's calls: no day counter; the bar at the top; "bonus points for
shifting it downwards when Chunky is running since they overlap today".

**What is new**: what lies beyond the bar's view is pinned to its ends at half strength rather
than dropped, as vanilla's own locator bar does; places carry an item sprite over a tinted dot
and fade in over the last 64 blocks of a 256-block range; the bar moves below any boss bar that
is showing; and every place but the players comes through the AzimuthLocation protocol
(D-0002), the compass and death points included, so the atlas is one implementer among any.

**What is not cloned, yet**: team colours on heads, dots sized by distance, the day counter, the
lodestone-compass management screen. Each is a small addition if wanted.

Rejected: a mixin into the Locator Bar's private waypoint list (their styling for free, no fade,
text symbols instead of icons, a patch into GPL code); drawing our markers over their bar from
their public config getters (no mixin, but their geometry copied by hand and still no place for
the atlas to plug in); an upstream API pull request (right in principle, slow, and the bar would
still be theirs).

Pack consequence: Azimuth replaces the Locator Bar's override jar on both sides in the pack
release that carries it, together with the Magical Map release that implements the protocol.
