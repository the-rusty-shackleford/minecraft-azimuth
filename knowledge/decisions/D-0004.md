# D-0004 — The bar sits below what vanilla draws, not below every boss event

Accepted 2026-09-24, the evening 1.0.0 went live. Rusty: "The bar is way too low. I only want it
lowered if a chunky is running, and one is not running right now … There no other bars
interfering with it, its just too low."

The bar learns how low the boss bars reach from NeoForge's `BossEventProgress` event, fired
once per boss event the client holds. 1.0.0 listened at normal priority and counted every one.
On the box, Block Factory's Bosses creates a boss event for **every vanilla warden a player
tracks** (`PlayerEvent.StartTracking` → `ServerBossEvent.addPlayer`) and, at the lowest
priority on the client, cancels vanilla's drawing of it to draw its own style or nothing. Rusty
stood 117 blocks from a warden in the deep dark below (Jdrum12, the only player online, warden at
y −50): their client held a boss event nothing drew at the top, Azimuth counted it, and the bar
sat 30 pixels down under empty sky. Mowzie's Mobs does the same for its bosses.

**The rule**: the bar sits below the boss bars vanilla actually draws. The listener runs at
`EventPriority.LOWEST`, receives cancelled events, and ignores any that is cancelled. A Chunky
pregeneration bar is a plain command boss bar nobody cancels, so it still pushes the bar down; a
warden's hidden bar, a Mowzie boss's restyled bar, do not. A mod that draws its own bar at the
top after cancelling the event will be overlapped; that is what Rusty asked for, and the
alternative (guessing which cancelled bars are drawn somewhere) cannot be known from the event.

Limit: a mod that also listens at LOWEST and registered after Azimuth cancels after we count.
Azimuth registers at client setup, after every mod's constructor-time subscribers, so the mods
met so far all run first.

Rejected: a name filter for Chunky's bar (a config nobody would find, and raids, the wither and
the dragon should push the bar down too); counting by the event's increment alone (a mod that
hides a bar can leave the increment as it was).

Verified by the booth: a boss bar pushes the bar down; a listener at HIGH priority that cancels
the event, standing in for such a mod, brings it back to the top while the boss event still
exists; removing the boss bar leaves it there.
