# D-0006 — A compass counts wherever the viewer carries it, their bags included

Accepted 2026-09-28. Part of Rusty's one change for every mod that looks at a player's inventory
("all things that see your inventory also see your backpack"): the Carried protocol
(`minecraft-carried`, its D-0001), nested in this jar.

- `azimuth:compass` reads every carried stack through `Carried.forEach`: the inventory's main
  slots, the offhand and every carried bag's storage cells (1.0.2 walked the inventory's 41 slots,
  armour included, where a compass cannot be worn anyway). An inventory compass keeps its id and
  colour by slot (`slot/3`); one in a bag is named by the bag and cell
  (`backpacksplus:slot/20/2`) and coloured by them.
- `azimuth:death` asks `Carried.has(player, recovery compass)`.

A compass in a bag does not tick, so a lodestone destroyed while its compass is in a bag stays on
the bar until the compass comes out and forgets it, as a compass in a chest would.

Verified: the compass GameTest, extended on its own player with a Backpacks+ bag in a main slot
(Backpacks+ 0.6.0 on the gametest server): the bag's lodestone compass on the bar under its bag's
id, the bag's recovery compass showing the death point. Failed on 1.0.2 (no bearing). An earlier
version put it on a new player, which the players test, counting every player on the server since
D-0005, then saw: the checks moved onto the existing player.
