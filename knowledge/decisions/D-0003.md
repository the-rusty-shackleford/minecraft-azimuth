# D-0003 — The bar's look: our own pixel art in the grammar players know

Accepted 2026-09-24.

Rusty, on the first build's bar next to the Locator Bar it replaces: "plain and shitty, a visual
downgrade". True by eye: a flat translucent slab, the letters N/E/S/W in the plain font, sprites
on solid tinted squares, unframed heads, and markers piling up at the ends at half strength. The
Locator Bar draws an outlined rounded bar, coloured outlined badges for the directions, outlined
dots, framed heads; that is what players' eyes are used to.

**What was chosen**, from the side-by-side mockup Rusty approved: an outlined rounded dark bar
with a top highlight, a shadow row and a notch at the viewer's heading; 7 px badges for north,
east, south and west with white letters on the colours people already know (red N, yellow E,
blue S, green W); each player's head in a 1 px black frame while within the fade, and a small
outlined dot once beyond it; each place's item sprite over an outlined dot in the place's
colour; and a chevron at either end of the bar for whatever lies outside the view on that side,
in the colour of the nearest such marker, instead of the dimmed markers pinned there before.
Markers keep six pixels clear at each end for the chevrons. The first booth photo stacked one
chevron per hidden marker two pixels apart; three of them hid each other and crowded the end
next to a far player's dot, so one per end it is: the chevron says something lies that way, its
colour says what is nearest. Markers are drawn farthest first, so where they overlap the
nearest is on top: Magical Map's booth, with five places within 20° of each other, had the
village underfoot hidden under a boat 92 blocks off while the bar drew nearest first.

**Every sprite is ours**, drawn by `devtools/art/sprites.py` into
`assets/azimuth/textures/gui/sprites/` and stitched into the game's GUI atlas; nothing is taken
from the Locator Bar's jar, which is GPL and would bind this AGPL mod's art to its licence and
its authors' terms. The tinted shapes (dots, chevrons) are white with a black outline, so the
client multiplies the colour in and the outline stays black: one sprite serves every colour.

**Amends D-0001**: what lies beyond the view is a chevron at the nearer end, not the marker
itself at half strength; and a player beyond the fade's floor is a small dot, not a faded head,
so a far player is told apart from a near one by shape as well as by strength. The fade
distances are unchanged and still travel with the payload.

Rejected: copying the Locator Bar's textures (GPL); drawing the bar with filled rectangles and
the font (what was there, and what Rusty judged); a larger bar (the Locator Bar's proportions are
what the pack's players know, and `bar.scale` is there for anyone who wants it bigger).

Verified by the booth: the bar photographed with a framed head, a far player's dot, the S badge
and the notch dead ahead, a bell and a campfire over their dots, and a chevron for the chest,
the compass point and a player all behind, judged at 4x against the mockup's proposed row.
